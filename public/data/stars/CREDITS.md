# Nearby stars — data credits

`nearby-stars.bin` and `named-stars.json` are generated by `npm run fetch:stars`
(`scripts/fetchNearbyStars.mjs`) from these public services. Do not edit them
by hand; regenerate them.

- **Hipparcos**, new reduction: van Leeuwen, F. 2007, A&A 474, 653 (VizieR
  I/311), joined to the original catalogue, ESA 1997, ESA SP-1200 (VizieR
  I/239), for V magnitudes and spectral types. Stars within 1 kpc whose
  parallax is measured to better than 20%.
- **VizieR** catalogue access tool, CDS, Strasbourg, France
  (DOI 10.26093/cds/vizier).
- **SIMBAD** database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France (Wenger et al.
  2000, A&AS 143, 9), for proper names and Bayer/Flamsteed designations.
- **NASA Exoplanet Archive**, operated by the California Institute of
  Technology under contract with NASA under the Exoplanet Exploration Program
  (Planetary Systems Composite Parameters, DOI 10.26133/NEA13), for planet
  hosts within 1 kpc. Hosts too faint for Hipparcos are placed from the
  archive's own position, distance and magnitude.

Colours come from B−V (Ballesteros 2012) through a blackbody fit; absolute
magnitudes from V and the parallax distance.
