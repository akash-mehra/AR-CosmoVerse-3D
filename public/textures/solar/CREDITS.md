# Planet textures

`sun.jpg`, `mercury.jpg`, `venus.jpg`, `earth.jpg`, `earth_clouds.jpg`,
`mars.jpg`, `jupiter.jpg`, `saturn.jpg`, `uranus.jpg` and `neptune.jpg` are the 2k texture maps by
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), based on NASA
imagery, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were retrieved
from Wikimedia Commons and resized to 1024×512; `earth_clouds.jpg` was also
converted to greyscale for use as an alpha map.

Any body without a file here (and Saturn's rings, and the starfield) is drawn
procedurally in `src/solar/SolarSystem.js`.
